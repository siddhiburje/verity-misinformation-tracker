Verity pulls apart a piece of text, a screenshot, or a live news story and traces its lineage — which earlier claims it resembles, how the wording drifted, and where the narrative is headed next. Built for a research project on misinformation evolution, powered by real similarity scoring and clustering, not canned demo data.

the pipeline

From a raw claim to a traced narrative

01

Collect

Paste text, drop an image, or search live news for a topic.

02

Extract

OCR pulls text out of screenshots; NewsAPI pulls in recent articles.

03

Analyze

Keywords, named entities, and sentiment are computed with real NLP.

04

Compare

TF-IDF similarity scores the claim against everything already tracked.

05

Cluster

Matching claims are grouped into a shared narrative, or flagged as new.

06

Visualize

An interactive graph and timeline show how the story evolved.

what you can feed it

Three ways in, one graph out

Text & claims

Paste an article, a headline, or a single sentence you saw shared online. Verity runs it through the same pipeline as everything else in the dataset.

Screenshots

Upload a screenshot of a post or article. OCR extracts the text and shows you exactly what it read, before analysis starts.

Live news topics

Search a topic and Verity pulls recent coverage through NewsAPI, then ingests each article into the same evolving graph.
